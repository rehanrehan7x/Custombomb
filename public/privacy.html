ThumbGrab Privacy Policy

This page explains, in plain language, how ThumbGrab handles information when you use the website.

Information You Provide

The thumbnail size checker processes a selected image in your browser. The page is designed to read the image locally so it can display dimensions and file information.

YouTube URLs

When you use the downloader, your browser requests thumbnail images from YouTube's image infrastructure and may request an image through ThumbGrab's download endpoint. Do not enter passwords, private credentials or other sensitive information into the tool.

Advertising

ThumbGrab may display Google advertising. Advertising providers can use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and the choices available to users. Google may personalize ads based on applicable settings and consent requirements.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Third-party advertising and analytics services, if enabled, may use cookies or similar technologies. Your browser also maintains ordinary technical information needed to load web pages.

Third-Party Services

ThumbGrab links to or requests resources from third-party services such as YouTube's image delivery infrastructure and Google advertising services. Their handling of data is governed by their respective policies.

Contact

For website questions, use the contact method provided by the site owner where available. Do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive credentials.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the website's features or advertising configuration changes.
