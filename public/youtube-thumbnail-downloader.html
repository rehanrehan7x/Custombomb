YouTube Thumbnail Downloader

Use ThumbGrab to download an available YouTube thumbnail online without installing software or creating an account.

How to download a YouTube thumbnail

  1. Open the YouTube video whose thumbnail you want to save.
  2. Copy the public video URL from your browser or YouTube app.
  3. Open the ThumbGrab YouTube Thumbnail Downloader.
  4. Paste the URL and select Get Thumbnail.
  5. Preview the available image versions and click Download.

Supported YouTube URLs

ThumbGrab supports common YouTube watch links, short youtu.be links, supported Shorts URLs, embed links and live links. The video must expose a publicly available thumbnail image.

Does ThumbGrab improve image quality?

No. The downloader displays available source thumbnail variants. It does not artificially upscale an image and present the result as an original higher-resolution thumbnail.
