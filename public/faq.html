YouTube Thumbnail Downloader FAQ

Is ThumbGrab free?

Yes. ThumbGrab is a free online YouTube thumbnail downloader and does not require an account.

How do I download a YouTube thumbnail?

Copy a public YouTube video URL, paste it into ThumbGrab, preview the available versions and click Download.

Does ThumbGrab support YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Supported YouTube Shorts URLs can be entered into the downloader.

Which thumbnail quality should I choose?

Choose the highest available version if you need the largest source image. If it is unavailable, choose another available variant.

Does ThumbGrab create 4K thumbnails?

No. The tool does not artificially upscale a smaller image and label it as an original 4K thumbnail.
