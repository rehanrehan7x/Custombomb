How to Download a YouTube Thumbnail

A simple mobile and desktop tutorial for saving a publicly available YouTube video thumbnail with ThumbGrab.

Advertisement

Method 1: Download a YouTube Thumbnail on Mobile

  1. Open the YouTube app or mobile browser.
  2. Open the video or Short you want to reference.
  3. Tap Share and copy the video link.
  4. Return to ThumbGrab and paste the link into the downloader.
  5. Tap Get Thumbnail and wait for the available versions.
  6. Choose a download option and save the image using your browser's download controls.

Method 2: Download on a Computer

  1. Open YouTube in Chrome, Edge, Safari or another modern browser.
  2. Copy the URL from the address bar.
  3. Paste it into the ThumbGrab YouTube Thumbnail Downloader.
  4. Review the preview and available quality choices.
  5. Click Download next to the image version you want.

Can I Download a YouTube Thumbnail in 4K?

The phrase “YouTube thumbnail 4K” is widely searched, but actual thumbnail resolution depends on what the source makes available. ThumbGrab checks the maximum available thumbnail variant rather than artificially upscaling a smaller image. If the source is not a true 4K image, the tool does not pretend that it is.

How to Download a YouTube Shorts Thumbnail

Copy the Shorts URL and paste it into ThumbGrab. A supported Shorts URL contains a video identifier that the tool can extract. The downloader then checks common thumbnail variants associated with that video.

How to Check the Downloaded Image

Use the Thumbnail Size Checker to see the image's exact width, height, aspect ratio, format and file size. This is useful if you want to verify a thumbnail before adding it to a design.

Common Problems

“Invalid URL”

Make sure you copied the full YouTube link and that it belongs to a supported URL format.

“Thumbnail not found”

The video may be private, deleted, restricted, unavailable or missing the requested thumbnail variant.

The image is not as large as expected

Thumbnail resolution is controlled by the source. A downloader cannot guarantee a 4K source if YouTube has not provided one.

Responsible Use

Only reuse thumbnails when you have permission or another lawful basis. A public URL does not automatically give you copyright ownership of the image.
