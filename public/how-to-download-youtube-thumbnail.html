How to Download a YouTube Thumbnail

You can save an available YouTube thumbnail in a few steps using a public video URL.

Step 1: Copy the YouTube URL

Open the video and copy its URL. A normal YouTube watch URL, youtu.be link or supported Shorts link can be used.

Step 2: Paste the URL into ThumbGrab

Go to the YouTube Thumbnail Downloader and paste the URL into the input field.

Step 3: Check the available thumbnail

ThumbGrab checks several common thumbnail image variants and displays the available versions.

Step 4: Download the image

Select Download next to the thumbnail version you want to save.

Quality note

The available resolution depends on the source thumbnail. ThumbGrab does not claim a resolution that is not actually available.
