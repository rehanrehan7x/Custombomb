How to Get a YouTube Transcript

  1. Copy the URL of a public YouTube video.
  2. Paste it into the box above.
  3. Click Get Transcript.
  4. Read the transcript beside the video or copy the text for notes and research.

Transcript availability depends on captions or subtitles being publicly available for the video.

YouTube Transcript FAQ

Can ThumbGrab transcribe videos without captions?

When the transcript provider is configured, ThumbGrab can use available captions first and an automatic speech recognition fallback when captions are unavailable.

Can I search and copy the transcript?

Yes. Search the transcript, copy timestamped text, or copy the plain transcript from the results panel.

Does the transcript include timestamps?

Yes. Timestamped transcript segments can be clicked to jump to the corresponding point in the YouTube player.
